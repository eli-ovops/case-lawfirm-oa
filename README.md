# Law-firm office system

An office system customised around the day-to-day work of different roles at a law firm, delivered with user training.

## Background

The firm needed its office workflows and scattered information organised around how staff actually worked, rather than a generic set of screens.

## My role

I developed the OA system from the team's on-site interviews and research, tailored the functions to the needs of different roles, and worked with the team on training and delivery.

## Architecture / Tech stack

The system was organised around role-specific office workflows and supporting information. A detailed implementation stack is not specified in this public case study.

## Hard parts

- **Turning field research into useful functions.** I used the team's on-site findings to develop functions around each role's actual office tasks.
- **Making the system understandable at handover.** I worked on the supporting product documentation and coordinated user training alongside delivery.

## Outcome

System development, user training and delivery were completed. This case study does not assign a numerical efficiency improvement to that delivery.
